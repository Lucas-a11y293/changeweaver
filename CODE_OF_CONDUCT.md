# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in ChangeWeaver a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include using welcoming and inclusive language, respecting differing viewpoints, accepting constructive criticism, and focusing on what is best for the project and its users. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, public or private intimidation, publishing private information without permission, and other conduct that would be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code. They may remove or edit comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate it, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for unacceptable behavior.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior through the private security contact described in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md). All reports will be reviewed and handled respectfully and confidentially.
